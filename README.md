## Overview
This is a major expansion mod that adds ocean content to RimWorld, centered around 4 vehicles created using the [Vehicle Map Framework](https://github.com/oelsart/VehicleMapFramework).

## Recommended DLC & Mod
- Odyssey
The tile where the deserted island was discovered will remain as a landmark even after the quest is complete.
- Royalty
Some research projects require a techprint, and the balance is set with that in mind.
- [Worldbuilder](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3522102833)
We recommend playing in a custom world with lots of water rather than one with a terrain consisting mostly of land.
- [As above, So below 2](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3776015553)
One option for ensuring space on a cramped ship.
